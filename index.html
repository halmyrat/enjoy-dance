How to play

  1. Prop the iPhone or iPad up at about waist height.
  2. Step back about two big steps, until your whole body fits the outline on screen.
  3. Pick a dance and copy the moves. Stars appear as you match them.

Grown-ups: please clear some space so nobody bumps into anything.

Common questions

The game says it can't see me.
Move back until your head and feet are both in the picture, and make sure the room is well lit. Plain backgrounds work best.

The camera doesn't start.
Open the Settings app, find Enjoy Dance, and turn on Camera. The game also needs an internet connection to load the dance camera.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, and the game has no ads or in-app purchases.

Where are scores saved?
Best scores and stars are saved on the device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
