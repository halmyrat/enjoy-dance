Enjoy Dance Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Prodigy Kids Inc.

Enjoy Dance is a dance game for children and families. We built it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • We don't ask for names, email addresses, birthdays, photos or any other personal details.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics in the app.
  • The camera is only used while a dance is playing, to follow body movement and score the moves.
  • Scores and stars are saved only on your device.

Camera

When a dance starts, the app asks for permission to use the camera. The camera picture is used to detect body position (for example, where the arms and legs are) so the game can tell whether a move was copied. Motion tracking is provided by our technology partner KinesteX, whose tracking component runs inside the app and is loaded from KinesteX's servers. Enjoy Dance itself does not record, save or upload camera pictures or video; it only receives the detected body positions while a dance is playing, and does not keep them afterwards. You can turn camera access off at any time in the device's Settings app; the game can't be played without it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the best score and number of stars for each dance, and a random player code (for example kid- followed by random letters and digits). This information stays on the device and is deleted when the app is deleted.

Information shared with service providers

  • KinesteX (motion tracking) receives the random player code and our app's license key so the tracking service can run. The code isn't linked to a name or any personal information. KinesteX's handling of data is described in the Privacy and Data Protection section of its Terms of Use.

We don't sell information, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Children's privacy

Enjoy Dance is designed for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
